k-Anonymity & DPDP Privacy Guarantee (k ≥ 10)

To prevent individual re-identification and peer stigma, student groups with fewer than 10 members are strictly suppressed and merged into an aggregate "Other" pool. If the aggregated pool remains below the threshold, it is fully concealed. No individual fitness scores, BMI, video feeds, or personal identifiers are ever visible on this portal.
