## 1. Scope & Medical Disclaimer
All nutritional values provided in KhelSetu are **approximate reference values** intended to assist collegiate students in building balanced nutritional awareness and fueling their physical activities. 

> **Important Disclaimer**:  
> Nutritional estimations from photos are not clinical assessments and must not be used for medical dietary prescriptions, diabetes management, or eating disorder diagnosis.

## 3. Portion Estimation Strategy
1. **Visual Segmentation**: AI Vision model identifies primary and secondary food items visible on standard collegiate mess trays, plates, or bowls.
2. **Standard Volume Priors**: Estimates base volume against standard reference vessels (katori = 150ml, plate = 25cm diameter, glass = 200ml).
3. **User Confirmation**: The user is *always* shown editable portion sliders and item tags before the meal is added to their nutrition log.
