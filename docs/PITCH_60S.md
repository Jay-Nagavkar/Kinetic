# KhelSetu: 60-Second Verbal Pitch

*Word Count: ~150 words | Speaking Duration: ~55–60 seconds*

---

> "Respected judges, nearly half of Indian adults are insufficiently active, rising from 22% in 2000 to 49.4% in 2022 according to *The Lancet*. In colleges, students know fitness matters, but academic fatigue, dorm room privacy fears, and intimidating gym culture hold them back. Meanwhile, college sports departments have zero visibility into non-athlete student health.
>
> Meet **KhelSetu** — the offline-first collegiate fitness bridge.
>
> It starts with **FitCheck**: a 5-minute private self-assessment using on-device computer vision. Video *never* leaves the student's phone, and our **Silhouette Mode** shows only an animated skeleton. It sets a personalized weekly goal scaling toward the WHO 150-minute benchmark.
>
> Next, **Fair-Play Squads**: teams of 4 to 8 students compete based on the *average percentage of their personal goals achieved* — so a beginner working out 60 minutes contributes just as much as an athlete doing 150.
>
> Finally, our **Campus Observatory** provides sports directors with $k$-anonymous aggregate insights without ever seeing private student records.
>
> Built on lightweight WebAssembly edge AI, KhelSetu works 100% offline and costs almost nothing to host. Thank you!"
