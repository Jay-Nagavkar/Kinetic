# KhelSetu Community Guidelines (SIH26196)

*Last updated: October 2026*

KhelSetu is an inclusive, privacy-first collegiate fitness and physical literacy platform. Our goal is to encourage daily movement, teamwork, and wellness in a supportive, zero-toxicity campus environment.

---

## 1. Respectful Communication
1. **Zero Tolerance for Harassment & Bullying**: We do not tolerate hate speech, slurs, derogatory comments, sexually explicit language, or personal attacks against peers, squad members, or sports committee officers.
2. **Body Positivity & Zero Body Shaming**: No comments criticizing anyone's physique, appearance, workout pace, or dietary choices. Fitness in KhelSetu is measured relative to personal baseline goals—not raw aesthetics or comparative metrics.
3. **No External Links / Spam**: To protect student safety and prevent commercial solicitation, external URLs are strictly blocked in peer channels and photo posts. Only approved institutional domains (`.ac.in`, `.edu.in`, `.gov.in`) may be shared by committee officers.

---

## 2. Photo & Feed Guidelines
1. **User-Controlled Uploads**: Photos uploaded to the Gym-Photo Feed or Meal Tracker are entirely voluntary. FitCheck camera frames and pose-estimation video **never leave your device**.
2. **Appropriate Imagery**: Only sports, gym activity, outdoor recreation, milestone celebrations, and food/meal plates should be shared. Nudity, obscene gestures, unauthorized photos of peers without their consent, or dangerous activities are prohibited.
3. **Automated Metadata Stripping**: All uploaded images undergo automated server-side EXIF stripping—GPS coordinates, device serial numbers, and camera metadata are scrubbed before storage.
4. **Visibility Control**: You choose who sees your posts (Squad Only, Hostel/Department, or Campus-Wide).

---

## 3. Crisis & Mental Health Support
If you or someone in your squad is experiencing emotional distress, academic stress, or a mental health crisis, please reach out for help. 
- You can contact your campus student welfare / counselling centre.
- Messages containing crisis keywords trigger automated support notices and alerts for sports welfare officers.

---

## 4. Moderation, Reporting & Retention
1. **Reporting Content**: Any student can report inappropriate chat messages or feed posts with a single tap.
2. **Automated Takedowns**: Posts receiving multiple community reports or containing abusive patterns are immediately held for review by institutional moderators.
3. **Right to Erasure (DPDP Act)**: You can delete your posts, comments, and uploaded photos at any time. When you trigger "Delete Account", all your posts, photos, and chat messages are permanently expunged from database and disk.
4. **Retention Window**: Media files and chat histories are subject to institutional retention purges (default 90 days).
